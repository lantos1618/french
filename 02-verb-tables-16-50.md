# Verbs 16–50 — combined tables

Same layout: **Past** (passé composé) · **Present** · **Future**, persons down the side.
Future endings are always **-ai/-as/-a/-ons/-ez/-ont** on the stem shown.
⚠️ = être-verb (participle agrees: resté(e)(s), arrivé(e)(s), etc.).
